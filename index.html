Magnus Carlsen is the best chess player in the world currently and is arguably the greatest chess player of all time!

Magnus Carlsen was born in Norway and became Grandmaster (GM) at only 12 years old. He has won World Chess Championship multiple times.

Greatest Achievement of Magnus

  • Magnus Carlsen has won 21 world championship
  • Became GM at 12 years of age
  • Peak FIDE is 2882 (Classical)
  • Holding the FIDE World No. 1 ranking consecutively since July 2011
